    Our guardian

    Meet Vyom.

    व्योम: the sky.

    Vyom watches over the Ashtadeep family, drawn in the style of the Indian comics many of us grew up reading. Around him circle the tools our apps are made of.

    He stands for the eight promises every Ashtadeep app keeps: yours stays yours, only true claims, and no dark patterns.

    Eight lamps. Eight promises.

    Every app we make keeps all eight.

    1 / 8
    • १

      Yours stays yours

      Our apps make their decisions on your device wherever they can, so what you do stays with you.

    • २

      Only true claims

      We say what we have tested, and nothing more.

    • ३

      No dark patterns

      No tricks to keep you scrolling and no traps to keep you paying.

    • ४

      Security first

      An app ships only after it passes its security checks.

    • ५

      Everywhere you are

      Phones, tablets, computers and browsers, released one at a time as each one is tested.

    • ६

      Light and fast

      Small apps that are careful with your battery and your data.

    • ७

      Every culture welcome

      Made for people of every language, culture and faith.

    • ८

      People over metrics

      We count lives made easier, not minutes spent in our apps.

    Our name

    सॉफ्टवेयर जो उजाला दे, शोर नहीं।

    ashta, eight · deep, lamp

    A diya does one quiet job. It gives light and asks for nothing back. We named the studio after eight of them, one for each promise we keep.

    तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय
