Eight promises.

Every app we make keeps all eight.

  • १

    Yours stays yours

    Our apps make their decisions on your device wherever they can, so what you do stays with you.

  • २

    Only true claims

    We say what we have tested, and nothing more.

  • ३

    No dark patterns

    No tricks to keep you scrolling and no traps to keep you paying.

  • ४

    Security first

    An app ships only after it passes its security checks.

  • ५

    Everywhere you are

    Phones, tablets, computers and browsers, released one at a time as each one is tested.

  • ६

    Light and fast

    Small apps that are careful with your battery and your data.

  • ७

    Every culture welcome

    Made for people of every language, culture and faith.

  • ८

    People over metrics

    We count lives made easier, not minutes spent in our apps.
